Roloe

A business card that brings your work.

Tap, and the person in front of you gets the help you already have.

Two people tap cards. Each person's own assistant reads the overlapping parts of the other's shared work and returns at most three findings, each credited to a moment in the giver's work, in the giver's own words. This server runs no model and holds no one's login.

Source and setup: github.com/WestonBDev/roloe
