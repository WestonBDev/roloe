Roloe

A business card that brings your work.

Scan, and the person in front of you gets the help you already have.

One person scans the other's card. Each person's own assistant reads the overlapping parts of the other's shared work and returns at most three findings, each credited to a moment in the giver's work, in the giver's own words. This server runs no model and holds no one's login.

Try it

To get started, scan this with your phone camera.

It opens Weston's demo card. Paste a GitHub repo link or type a question, and his assistant answers in about 20 seconds. No app, no account. On a laptop, open the demo card instead.

Source and setup: github.com/WestonBDev/roloe
